NeonTicTac: Victory Awaits 🎮✨
Welcome to NeonTicTac: Victory Awaits, an electrifying twist on the timeless classic Tic Tac Toe! ⚡ With vibrant animations, glowing effects, and a thrilling gaming experience, this version is guaranteed to bring endless fun. Whether you're playing against a friend or challenging the AI, every move is a visual delight! ✨

Features 🌟
Customizable Grid Sizes: Choose from 3x3, 4x4, or 5x5 grids for endless fun! 🔲
AI vs Player Mode: Test your skills against a smart AI 🤖 or challenge a friend in a local match! 👯‍♂️
Glowing Visuals: Enjoy dazzling animations and glowing effects ✨ with every move.
Confetti Celebration: Winning has never been more fun! Celebrate your victory with a shower of confetti 🎉.
Smooth Gameplay: Easy-to-play with intuitive controls and responsive UI. 🌟
Engaging Soundtrack: Play with an immersive background score that keeps you in the game! 🎶
How to Play 🎮
Start the Game: Choose your grid size (3x3, 4x4, or 5x5).
Select Your Mode: Play against a friend or challenge the AI. 🤖
Choose Your Symbol: Pick either X or O and get ready to make your mark! ❌⭕
Make Your Move: Take turns to place your symbol on the grid, and aim for victory! 🏆
Celebrate: Watch as confetti rains down when you win! 🎉
Technologies Used 🚀
HTML5: For structure and layout of the game.
CSS3: Styling with stunning animations and effects to make the game visually dynamic. 🎨
JavaScript: Game logic, interactive features, and AI functionality to enhance your playing experience. 🤖
